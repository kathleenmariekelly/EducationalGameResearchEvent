Room Architect

Build a layout of rooms, then navigate it. Learn how directed & undirected graphs really work.

Tool

Levels

    Room tray

    Each room has a fixed budget of paths: → one-way-out and — two-way. Click a room, then a grid cell to place it. Click a placed room to pick it up.

    How to play

    1. Arrange every room from the tray onto the board.
    2. Link one-way: click source room, then target room (uses one → port on the source).
    3. Link two-way: click two rooms (uses one — port on each).
    4. Hit Run. Move along your links, respecting arrows. Collect all items to unlock the Exit.
