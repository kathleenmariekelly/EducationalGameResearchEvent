DISCRETE QUEST

A Chaos Curse has unleashed monsters across the land. Only a mage who masters discrete mathematics can lift it!

🗺️ Explore the biomes with the W A S D keys (arrows too).
Meadow, Desert, Tundra, Cavern, Swamp & Volcano each
hide different monsters.

👾 Walk into a monster to start a battle.

🔮 Pick one of 5 spells — 🔮 Arcane, 🔥 Fireball,
❄️ Frost, ⚡ Volt, 🌿 Bramble — then solve its
discrete-math problem to cast it. Match the monster's
weakness for a critical hit!

🧪 Monsters drop items — open Craft Potions to brew
healing potions & permanent buffs.

👑 Clear every monster in a biome to summon its boss,
who drops rare materials for legendary recipes.

📜 Check the Quest Log for your main quest & side quests
— complete them for rewards!

🏆 Break the Chaos Curse by defeating the Inferno Lord!
