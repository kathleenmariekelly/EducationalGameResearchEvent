How to Play

Goal: invaders spawn at the green gate and march toward your keep 🏰. Place your cute soldiers to block and reroute the enemies AND attack any that wander within range. Each enemy has a health bar — whittle it down before they reach your keep. Enemies get tougher every wave!

Painting the map

  • 🐾 Soldier — the only blocker: enemies path around it, so use soldiers to reshape the route. Soldiers also shoot nearby enemies.
  • 🌿 Swamp — goes right on the path; enemies wade through at half speed (unlock: medium/hard question).
  • ☠️ Gas — goes right on the path; poisons enemies for damage over time (unlock: hard question).
  • ❄️ Frost — goes right on the path; freezes enemies to 40% speed so soldiers get more shots (unlock: medium/hard question).
  • Erase — remove a defense and refund its charge.

Key idea: only 🐾 soldiers block and reroute the path — that's where the pathfinding reacts. Swamp, gas, and frost are effects you lay on the path itself.

The algorithms (one per world)

  • BFS — finds the fewest steps. Ignores swamp cost entirely.
  • Dijkstra — finds the cheapest route, so it detours around swamp.
  • A* — Dijkstra plus a heuristic to search faster. A bad heuristic makes it wander — that's the lesson in admissibility.

Watch it think

The faint blue tiles show the search frontier — every cell the algorithm explored while finding the route. The gold line is the path it chose. Watch how each algorithm searches differently!
